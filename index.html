In development

Built with Claude

GOOD CONTENTS is an AI-native studio. Claude works across every stage of Samjoko, from research and design to engineering and testing, as our researcher, designer and engineer.

Game research

Claude studies the games and systems players love and distills them into documented design rules, with evidence behind every decision.

Character AI

We build AI-driven characters and enemy strategists with their own personalities, motives and tactics, so every encounter feels authored rather than scripted.

Simulation & testing

A deterministic game simulation lets Claude run large batches of automated battles to verify balance and catch regressions before a human ever plays.

Worlds at scale

A cast of more than a hundred characters, multilingual from day one, maintained as a living world wiki that drives the game directly.

Every line of code, design document and test in Samjoko is created in collaboration with Claude.
