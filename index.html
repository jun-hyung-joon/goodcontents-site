The game

A world on the move. Battles you plan, then watch unfold.

Samjoko takes its name from the three-legged sun crow of legend. It's a single-player strategy RPG about leading an army through a living world.

Concept illustration
I

Move in real time.

Your squads march across one continuous map, alongside rivals who don't wait for you. Where you stand when the fighting starts decides half the battle.

Concept illustration
II

Fight by plan.

Build each squad, set its formation and give its orders before contact. Then the battle runs on its own, turn by turn, and you see if the plan holds.

Concept illustration
III

Stay for hundreds of hours.

Nations, rivals and companions with their own reasons to fight. A long campaign with room to wander, recruit and make the world remember you.

The studio

We connect the things people love.

GOOD CONTENTS is a game studio. We look for the connection between games players already love, then build the game where they meet. AI works with us at every step. The vision stays ours.

Game research

Find the next combination.
