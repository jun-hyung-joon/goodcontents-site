How we build

AI is our partner at every stage of development.

From the first research note to the final test, AI works alongside us as a partner. Our team sets the vision. AI helps us reach it faster, test it harder and build it deeper than a studio our size ever could alone.

01 · GAME RESEARCH

New kinds of fun, from games players already love.

We study the games players love and connect them to one another, finding combinations that create a kind of fun no single game could offer on its own.

02 · CHARACTER AI

Worlds that draw you in from the first minute.

Characters with their own personalities, motives and tactics respond the way people would, so players can step into the world and feel immersed right away.

03 · SIMULATION

Testing around the clock for every 1% better.

Automated simulations play our games 24 hours a day, testing every change, because even a 1% improvement in how a game feels is worth chasing.

04 · WORLDS AT SCALE

Depth worth hundreds of hours.

Vast worlds with deep histories, factions and characters, built for single-player adventures that players can lose themselves in for hundreds of hours.
