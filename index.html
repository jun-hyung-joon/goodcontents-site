How Samjoko came together

Tactics × Open world × Auto battler × Hero's journey = Samjoko

Each of these is something players already love. Samjoko is what happens when they share one world.

What you do in Samjoko.

Move in real time.

Your squads march across one continuous map. Where they stand when the fighting starts decides a lot.

Fight by plan.

Set formations and orders before contact. Then the battle runs on its own, turn by turn.

Stay for the long run.

Nations, rivals and companions with their own reasons. A single-player campaign meant to last hundreds of hours.

We build with AI at every step.

AI works with us from the first research note to the last test. The vision stays ours.

Game research

Finding the next combination.

We study the games players love and look for pairings that create a kind of fun neither game has alone.

Character AI

Characters worth believing.

Allies and enemies act on their own personalities and motives, so the world is easy to step into.

Simulation

Tested around the clock.

Automated play runs 24 hours a day. If a change makes the game even 1% better, it stays.

World building

Room for hundreds of hours.

Histories, factions and characters with enough depth to carry a long single-player campaign.
