OVERVIEWThe world of Samjoko

A world on the move, and an army that answers to you.

Samjoko takes its name from the mythical three-legged sun crow. It is a single-player strategy RPG about leading an army through a living world: marching across open land, choosing your fights, and building the squads that will carry you to the end.
